# StackRead Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 14 October 2026*

StackRead is a file reader for Android. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use it.

## The short version

**StackRead does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your
personal data.** Everything stays on your device.

## Your files

- Files you add to StackRead - from the file picker, from other apps ("Open
  with" or "Share"), or from a folder or ZIP you import — are **copied into the
  app's private storage on your device**.
- Your library, your groups, your reading positions and your settings are
  stored **only on your device**.
- StackRead **never uploads** your files or any information about them.

## Data we collect

**None.** StackRead has:

- no account or sign-in;
- no analytics, tracking or advertising;
- no crash reporting that sends data off your device;
- no servers that receive your data.

## Backups

If you use **Back up library**, StackRead creates a backup file and hands it to
Android so **you** choose where it goes (for example, your Downloads folder or a
cloud drive of your choice). StackRead does not send the backup anywhere itself.
Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your library is
not copied to Google Drive without your action.

## Permissions

StackRead does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera
or microphone, and it does not request general access to your device storage.
It can only read the files you explicitly choose to open or import.

## Links

If a document contains a web link and you choose to open it, it opens in your
web browser. Whatever happens there is governed by that website and your
browser, not by StackRead.

## Children

StackRead is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
any information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Because all data lives on your device, you are always in control:

- Delete a file or group inside the app, or
- Uninstall StackRead, which removes all of its data from your device.
